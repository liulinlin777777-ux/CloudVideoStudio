# Roadmap

This roadmap records planned project direction. It is not a statement that a capability is implemented, tested, or available. P0 has a bounded prototype under PR verification; P1–P5 remain planned. See [P0 acceptance](docs/P0_ACCEPTANCE.md) and the actual CI check before inferring completion.

| Stage | Planned focus | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Core technology validation | Bounded prototype; PR/CI verification |
| P1 | Professional editing foundation | Planned |
| P2 | Professional post-production capabilities | Planned |
| P3 | AI-assisted editing | Planned |
| P4 | AI video generation and digital humans | Planned |
| P5 | Cloud batch production and operations | Planned |

## P0 — Core technology validation

Validate the technical foundations and constraints needed to choose an implementation direction.

## P1 — Professional editing foundation

Plan and build the foundation for professional non-linear timeline editing.

## P2 — Professional post-production capabilities

Plan and build the professional post-production capabilities that follow the editing foundation.

## P3 — AI-assisted editing

Explore AI-assisted editing workflows and their integration with the editing foundation.

## P4 — AI video generation and digital humans

Explore AI video generation and digital-human workflows as planned product capabilities.

## P5 — Cloud batch production and operations

Explore cloud-based batch production and operational workflows.

Scope, ordering, and timing may change through community discussion. No target release dates are set.
