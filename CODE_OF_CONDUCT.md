# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in CloudVideoStudio to be welcoming and respectful to people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and work; do not attack people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and make room for different perspectives.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's information or work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive conduct or other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to address unacceptable behavior. Reports should be sent privately to a project maintainer through GitHub rather than posted as a public Issue. Maintainers will handle reports as carefully as they can; no response time is promised.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
